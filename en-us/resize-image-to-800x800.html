What is 800 × 800 used for?

800 × 800 pixels is the standard size for marketplace and product listing photos. It is commonly used for:

  • Online marketplace listings (Amazon, eBay, Etsy and Daraz style square galleries)
  • E-commerce product photos and catalog images
  • Square thumbnails for shops and classified ads
  • Food menu and real-estate listing photos

Platforms and apps that ask for this size expect the file to match it exactly — a photo that is even a few pixels off may be cropped by the platform itself, rejected, or shown soft and blurry. Resizing to the exact pixel size first keeps you in control of how the image looks.

Cover, contain or stretch — which fit mode?

Your photo and the 800 × 800 frame rarely have the same shape, so something has to give. Cover scales the photo until the frame is completely filled and trims the overflow from the center — nothing is distorted and there are no empty bands, but the edges of the photo are cut away. Contain scales the photo until it fits completely inside the frame and fills the leftover space with the background color you choose — the whole photo stays visible, with bands on the sides or top and bottom. Stretch pulls the photo to exactly 800 × 800 in both directions; use it only when the exact pixel size matters more than correct proportions.

Worked example

Take a 3000 × 2000 px photo. In Cover mode it is scaled by 0.4 and the tool uses a 2000 × 2000 px slice from the center of the photo, so the result fills the whole 800 × 800 frame with nothing stretched. In Contain mode the same photo is scaled by 0.267 to 800 × 533 px and centered on a 800 × 800 canvas, leaving a 134 px band at the top and bottom filled with the background color you pick. Stretch mode simply forces the full photo into 800 × 800 px — exact size, but shapes change.

Frequently asked questions

Is this 800 × 800 resizer free?

Yes — completely free, with no signup, no watermark and no limit on how many images you resize.

What is the difference between Cover and Contain?

Cover fills the whole 800 × 800 frame and center-crops whatever does not fit, so no part of the frame is empty. Contain fits the entire photo inside the frame and fills the empty space with a background color, so nothing is cut off.

Will my image look distorted?

Only in Stretch mode, which changes the proportions on purpose. Cover and Contain always keep the original proportions of your photo.

Can I enlarge a smaller image to 800 × 800?

Yes, the tool will scale it up to exactly 800 × 800 px, but enlarging cannot add real detail, so a much smaller original may look soft. For icons and avatars a small upscale is usually fine.

Should I choose JPG or PNG?

JPG gives a smaller file and is best for photographs. PNG is lossless, keeps text and graphics perfectly sharp, and is the safer choice for icons and logos.

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No. Resizing happens 100% in your browser using the Canvas API. Your image never leaves your device.
