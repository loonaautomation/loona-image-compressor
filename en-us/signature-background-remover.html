About removing signature backgrounds honestly

Forms, contracts, applications and letters often need a signature on the document — but a photo of your signature comes with a rectangle of paper around it. This tool removes that paper: it estimates the paper color from the corners of your photo, then turns every pixel's distance from the paper color into transparency. Dark ink stays fully opaque, paper becomes fully transparent, and the soft edge pixels in between become partly transparent, so the signature blends naturally onto any document.

Please use it with honest expectations: this is not AI and it does not trace your signature. It works best on plain paper in even light. Hard shadows across the page, very faint pencil, or ink almost the same color as the paper will need the sliders — and sometimes simply a better photo. A signature on white paper photographed in daylight usually comes out clean in seconds. Only ever use your own signature, or one you have permission to use.

How it works

Your photo is drawn onto a canvas at its natural size. The tool averages the corner pixels to estimate the paper color, then measures how far every pixel is from that color. The Background removal slider sets the cut-off: pixels close to the paper color become transparent, pixels far from it stay as ink, with a smooth ramp in between so pen edges do not look bitten. The Cleanup control first flattens slow changes in brightness — the shading from a shadow or a bent page — by comparing each pixel with a blurred copy of the photo, and then removes tiny isolated specks. If you switch on pure black, the ink color is replaced with solid black while the transparency shape stays the same.

Worked example

A blue pen signature photographed on white paper: the corners average to about RGB 238, 236, 228 (the paper), while the pen strokes sit near RGB 40, 60, 140. At the default setting, the paper and its soft shadow (about RGB 205, 200, 188) fall inside the removal range and become transparent, while the pen — far from the paper color — stays fully opaque and keeps its blue. Switching on pure black turns the same shape into a solid black signature, ready for a printed form.

Frequently asked questions

Is this signature background remover free?

Yes — completely free, with no signup, no watermark and no limit on how many signatures you clean.

Is my signature uploaded anywhere?

No. All processing happens 100% in your browser using the Canvas API. Your signature image never leaves your device — which matters, because a signature is sensitive.

Why is some paper still visible around the ink?

The paper in that area is darker than the rest — usually a shadow or a crease. Raise the Background removal slider a little, or raise Cleanup to flatten the shading first.

Why did a faint part of my signature disappear?

That stroke is very light, so it looks like paper to the tool. Lower the Background removal slider until the stroke comes back, then add only as much Cleanup as you need.

Can I use it for a rubber stamp?

Yes — press the Stamp mode preset. It uses stronger removal and pure black ink, which suits most ink stamps on paper.

What format is the result?

A PNG with a real transparent background, at the same size as your photo. You can place it over any document, form or image in Word, PDF editors and office apps.
