What this enhancer can — and cannot — do

Let us be honest, because many “AI enhancer” pages are not. This tool improves a photo with classic, proven image processing — the same maths photo editors have relied on for decades. It can stretch dull, flat contrast so colours look alive again; sharpen edges so eyes, text and details look crisper; blend away light grain and noise; and enlarge a picture to 2× or 4× its size with high-quality smoothing, so it displays and prints better.

What it cannot do is invent detail that was never captured. If a face is a blur of a few pixels, no honest tool can turn it into a sharp portrait. True AI upscalers guess new detail using trained models on powerful servers — and even their results are partly invented. This enhancer never guesses: every pixel in your result comes from your own photo. In return it is completely free, works instantly on your device, and your photo is never uploaded to any server.

How it works

Auto Enhance first reads the photo’s histogram and stretches the darkest and lightest tones towards true black and white (auto levels), which lifts the grey “fog” of a dull shot. The sliders then adjust brightness, contrast and saturation pixel by pixel. Sharpening uses unsharp masking: a softly blurred copy is compared with the original, and the difference — the edges — is added back strengthened, so edges stand out. Noise cleanup blends each pixel gently towards the median of its neighbours, which removes speckle while keeping real edges. Upscaling resizes in steps of at most 2× with high-quality smoothing, which avoids the blocky look of one big jump.

Worked example

A pixel of value 120 whose blurred neighbourhood averages 100, with Sharpness at 50%, becomes 120 + 0.75 × (120 − 100) = 135 — the edge it sits on becomes visibly stronger. And an 800 × 600 photo upscaled 2× is saved at 1600 × 1200: four times the pixels, ready for a bigger print or screen.

Frequently asked questions

Is this photo enhancer free?

Yes — completely free, with no signup, no watermark and no limit on how many photos you enhance.

Is this an AI upscaler?

No — and we say so openly. It uses classic image processing (auto levels, unsharp masking, median noise cleanup and stepped resizing), not a trained AI model. It cannot invent detail that is not in your photo; what it does, it does honestly and privately on your device.

Can it fix a very blurry photo?

It can make a slightly soft photo look crisper, and upscaling makes it bigger — but heavy blur means the detail was never recorded, and no tool can truthfully bring it back. For a mildly soft phone photo, press Auto Enhance, then try Sharpness at 40–70%.

How is this different from Photo Filters & Fix?

Photo Filters & Fix is for quick colour corrections and ready presets. The Enhancer adds Auto Enhance (auto levels), strength-controlled sharpening, noise cleanup, the before/after compare slider and 2×/4× upscaling.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. All processing happens 100% in your browser, pixel by pixel. Your photo never leaves your device.

PNG or JPG — which should I download?

PNG keeps every pixel exactly (lossless) and suits further editing or printing. JPG gives a much smaller file for sharing, saved at high quality (92%).
