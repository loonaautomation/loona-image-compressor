Frequently Asked Questions

What size photo does RRB NTPC (Railways) require?

320 × 240 px, in a JPG of 30–70 KB, per the latest official notification. The tool exports exactly that size and fits the KB band.

Why do applications get rejected over uploads?

The most common causes for RRB NTPC (Railways): wrong pixel dimensions, files outside the KB band, old or filtered photos, and — for signatures — capital-letter signing in bank exams. This tool fixes the measurable ones; the rest is in the notes above.

My file is below the minimum KB — is that a problem?

Portals technically specify a band. If your result is under the minimum, the tool reports it honestly; in practice portals rarely reject small-but-legible files, but raising scanning quality (use a real photo of the paper, not a screenshot) brings most files into band.

Is my photo/signature uploaded to a server?

No — everything happens in your browser. Exam documents are personal; they should never sit on a stranger's server.

Should I re-check the size next cycle?

Yes. Bodies adjust bands between notifications — the figures here were checked on 9 November 2026 against the latest notification; always confirm in the current one before the last date.
