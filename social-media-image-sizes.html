Frequently Asked Questions

Why do sizes keep changing?

Platforms redesign feeds every year or two. This hub is re-checked against official help pages; the date above is the last check.

One design for all platforms?

Ratios differ (9:16 stories, 1.91:1 cards, 3:1 headers) — make each platform's version from your original with its maker rather than re-cropping another platform's export.
