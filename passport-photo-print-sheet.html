  1. Load your photo — drop it above or click to browse. Nothing is uploaded; the photo stays on your device.

About printing passport photos on A4

A photo studio charges per sheet, not per photo — yet a single A4 sheet can hold several passport photos at once. This tool builds that sheet for you at true print size: A4 at 300 DPI is exactly 2480 × 3508 pixels, and each 35 × 45 mm photo is 413 × 531 pixels (or 600 × 600 pixels for the US 2 × 2 inch size). The copies are centred on the page with even gaps, so the sheet looks balanced and is easy to cut with scissors or a paper trimmer.

Printing it yourself saves studio money: one A4 sheet of photo paper costs a fraction of a studio set, and you keep the digital sheet for reprints whenever an application asks for fresh copies. Use glossy or matte photo paper for the closest match to studio output, and let the print dry for a few minutes before cutting, so the ink does not smudge along the edges.

How it works

Your photo is cover-fitted into every cell — scaled so the cell is completely covered and centred, cropping any overflow. The layout maps 4 copies to 2 columns × 2 rows, 6 copies to 3 × 2 and 8 copies to 4 × 2. When you print from this page, the sheet is sent to the printer as a full A4 page with zero margins.

Worked example

With 8 copies at 35 × 45 mm, the sheet holds 4 columns × 2 rows of 413 × 531 px photos on the 2480 × 3508 px A4 canvas, centred with even gaps. Printed at 100% scale on A4, every cut photo measures exactly 35 × 45 mm — cut slowly along the thin guide borders.

Frequently asked questions

Is this print sheet maker free?

Yes — completely free, with no signup, no watermark and no limit on how many sheets you make.

How do I print the sheet correctly?

Print at 100% scale (actual size), never “fit to page”, on A4 paper with margins set to none. The Print button on this page already sets A4 with zero margins for you.

What paper should I use?

Photo paper — glossy or matte — gives the sharpest, most studio-like result. Plain paper works for test prints and rough copies.

Can I use any photo, not just a passport crop?

Yes. Any photo is cover-cropped into each cell, centred. For official use, start from a properly framed passport photo — try our free Passport Size Photo Maker first.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. The sheet is built 100% in your browser using the Canvas API. Your photo never leaves your device.

How many copies fit on one A4 sheet?

This tool offers 4, 6 or 8 copies in neat centred rows. Eight 35 × 45 mm photos fit comfortably on A4 with room to cut between them.
