Where 1024 × 1024 px comes from

App-icon and AI-image standard square. 1024×1024 is the default output of many AI image generators and the master size for app icons before scaling.

Worked example

Add your image above — the frame is already 1024 × 1024. Drag to choose which part survives the crop, zoom for a tighter frame, and download. If your source is a paper print size (for example 35 × 45 mm), the pixel size here is its 300-DPI twin: mm × 11.811 = px. Always confirm whether a portal asks for print pixels (300 DPI) or smaller screen pixels — the two differ by a factor of three.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 1024 × 1024 px used for?

App-icon and AI-image standard square. 1024×1024 is the default output of many AI image generators and the master size for app icons before scaling.

Will resizing distort my image?

No — the tool crops to the exact ratio first (you choose the crop by dragging/zooming), then exports at exactly 1024×1024 px. Nothing is stretched.

My image is smaller than the target — what happens?

It is enlarged to fit the frame, which softens it. The result line tells you the truth either way; for print targets, start from the largest original you have.

Which format should I pick?

JPG for photos and uploads to forms; PNG for graphics, screenshots and transparency.
