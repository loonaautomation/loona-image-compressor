About Loona Image Compressor

Free image tools, built by a teacher in Pakistan.

Loona Image Compressor is a free online image tool site from Loona Automation. It helps students, teachers, freelancers, shopkeepers, office workers and families finish a common, urgent job quickly: making an image the exact size, dimensions or format that a form, portal or website demands — without paying for software, creating an account, or handing over personal photographs.

What this site does

The headline tool compresses JPG, PNG and WebP images to an exact target size in kilobytes — not "about smaller", but the size the form demands, such as 50KB, 100KB or 200KB — and can process images in batches and hand them back as a single ZIP file. Around it sit resizers, a cropper, format converters between JPG, PNG, WebP and other formats, a passport and visa photo maker, a photo-to-PDF maker, and creative and checking tools such as a colour picker, palette generator and contrast checker. Open the homepage to use the compressor and browse the other tools.

Private by design

Every tool on this site runs entirely in your web browser. Your image files are processed locally on your own device and are never uploaded to any server. The photograph of your national identity card, passport or family never leaves your phone or computer, which is precisely why applicants trust this site at midnight before a deadline. Our Privacy Policy explains this in full, including how advertising and analytics cookies work on the pages themselves.

The story

Loona Automation was founded by Aurang Zeb Loona, a school teacher and AI teacher trainer from Jaranwala, in the Faisalabad district of Punjab, Pakistan. His working life has two halves. In the classroom, he teaches students and trains fellow teachers to use artificial intelligence in their own classrooms. Outside the classroom, he builds the tools he wished existed: first for his own students and colleagues, then for anyone on the internet who has the same problem. Loona Image Compressor began with a familiar frustration — a photograph that had to fit an admission or job form's strict kilobyte limit — and grew, one solved problem at a time, into this collection.

The mission: Bolti Duniya

Behind the tools sits a bigger mission called Bolti Duniya — "the speaking world." Its belief can be stated in one sentence: "Every book should speak — in every language — for everyone who cannot read." The free tools are how we reach people today; that mission is where we are heading.

Why is everything free?

Because the people who need these tools most are often the people least able to pay for them: a student compressing a photo for an admission form at midnight, a graduate preparing a visa photograph, a small seller shrinking product photos for a marketplace. Charging them per use would defeat the purpose. The site is funded by advertising. The tools stay free for everyone — no sign-up, no watermark and no trial that expires halfway through your task — and advertisers, through programmes such as Google AdSense, cover the costs of running and improving them.

How we work

· Free, no sign-up. If a task needs an account, it is the wrong design.
· Private by design. Wherever a job can be done in your browser, it is done there.
· Built for real conditions. Pages are kept light so they load quickly on inexpensive phones and slow connections.
· Corrected in the open. Readers report errors by email, and fixes go live at the same address.
