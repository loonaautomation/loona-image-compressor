Reporting a problem with a tool

The single most useful email we receive is "this tool gave me the wrong result" — but it only helps if we can reproduce the problem. If a tool misbehaves for you, please include: the tool's name or its web address; the type of image file you used (for example JPG or PNG) and its approximate size; what you expected to happen; and what happened instead. The device and browser you used (for example, "Android phone, Chrome") matter too, because some faults appear on one browser only. Please do not email us your personal photographs, identity documents, passwords, bank details or other secrets; we will never ask you for them, and the tools on this site work without sending your images anywhere.

What happens after you write

Your message lands in the Loona Automation inbox, which is checked on working days. Most messages get a personal reply within 1–2 working days. Tool bug reports are logged and fixed in order of how many people they affect; if you reported a bug, feel free to check the tool again after a few days — fixes go live on the same web address, with nothing to download or update.
