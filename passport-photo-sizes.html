Frequently Asked Questions

Why do countries use different sizes?

Photo sizes grew from national print traditions — 35×45 mm (ICAO Europe), 2×2 inch (US), 33×48 (China), 50×70 (Canada). Digital uploads reuse the same frames in pixels.

Can one photo serve two countries?

Only when both share the frame AND background AND head-size band. The table shows each document's exact rule so you can compare honestly.
