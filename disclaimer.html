What this site is — and is not

Loona Image Compressor is a set of 41 free image tools that run entirely in your web browser: compressors that shrink JPG, PNG and WebP files to an exact target size, converters, resizers, passport and exam photo makers and related photo utilities. It is a general-purpose toolset for everyday use. It is not an official service of any government, examination board, embassy or employer, and it is not affiliated with any of them. Using a tool here never, by itself, guarantees that any organisation will accept the file you prepare.

Processing happens on your device

Every tool works locally in your browser. Your images are never uploaded to a server, so we never see, store or pass them to anyone else. The work lives only in your open tab: if you close the page before downloading, the result is gone and we cannot recover it for you. Keep your original file until you have opened the finished image, checked it, and confirmed it works where you intend to use it. Backing up your own images remains your responsibility.

Compression and quality: no guarantee at extreme targets

Compression makes a file smaller by discarding image data, and there is a hard limit to how much can be discarded. Our exact-size tools search for the highest quality that fits the limit you chose, and reduce dimensions only when quality alone cannot reach it — but no tool can promise a sharp result at an extreme target. A detailed photograph forced under 10 KB or 20 KB will look soft. The smaller the target compared with the original, the more quality you should expect to lose. Always view the finished image at full size before relying on it; if it is not good enough, choose a larger target.

Passport, visa and exam photos: acceptance rests with the issuing authority

Our passport, visa and exam photo tools crop and size a photograph to the dimensions and file size you select, following commonly published specifications. Specifications change, and each authority applies its own current rules on dimensions, background, format and file size. Whether a photo is accepted is decided solely by the organisation that receives it — a passport office, an embassy, an examination board or an employer — never by this website. Before you submit, read the current requirements on the official form or website, and verify your finished file’s dimensions and size yourself. Treat our result as a prepared file that still needs your check, not an approved one.

Results vary with the source image

Two images with the same pixel dimensions can compress very differently. A plain signature scan may reach 10 KB easily, while a busy, detailed photograph of the same size may need its dimensions reduced to reach the same target. Examples quoted on our pages are illustrations from real files, not promises about yours.
