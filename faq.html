Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about cost, privacy, exact sizes and reporting problems.

Is Loona Image Compressor free?

Yes. All 41 tools are free, with no trial and no paid tier. The site is supported by advertising.

Do I need to sign up? Is there a watermark?

No sign-up is needed, and results carry no watermark. Open a tool, add your image and download the result.

Where do my images go?

Nowhere. Every tool runs inside your browser on your own device. Your images are never uploaded, stored or seen by us. Our Privacy Policy explains the standard analytics and advertising cookies used on the pages themselves — those cookies never receive your images.

Can I compress an image to an exact size, like 50KB or 100KB?

Yes — that is the headline tool. You choose the target size in kilobytes and the compressor adjusts the image to reach it. Because the work happens on your device, very large images depend on your device's available memory.

Will my passport or visa photo be accepted?

The photo makers prepare your picture to the dimensions you select, but acceptance is always decided by the authority receiving your application, under its own current rules. Check that organisation's requirements first, and verify the finished file before submitting. Our Disclaimer explains this.

What other tools are here?

41 tools in total: compressors to exact sizes, resizers, a cropper, converters between JPG, PNG and WebP, passport and visa photo makers, a photo-to-PDF maker, and checking tools such as a colour picker, palette generator and contrast checker.

How do I report an error or a broken tool?

Email loonaautomation@gmail.com or use the Feedback page. Include the tool's name, what happened, and your device and browser — please do not email us your personal photographs.
