The UK is the famous trap: its 35×45 mm size looks like the Schengen size, but the background must be light grey or cream, not white.

Rules that get UK Passport Photo photos rejected

  • Background must be plain light grey or cream — NOT white (the most common UK rejection)
  • No shadows on the face or behind the head
  • Mouth closed, eyes open, no smile requirement — a plain expression
  • Digital photos from a phone are accepted if unedited — no filters or beauty mode

Worked example — from phone photo to accepted upload

Start with a recent photo taken in daylight about 1.5 metres from a plain wall. Add it above: the frame is locked to the 35:45 ratio, so drag until your face sits in the middle, with your eyes around the upper third line and both shoulders visible. Zoom slightly rather than standing closer — it keeps the perspective natural. Download the 600 × 750 px JPG, check the reported KB against your portal's limit, and only then upload. Requirements change — always confirm the current specification with the HM Passport Office (gov.uk) before a final submission.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the official UK Passport Photo photo size?

35 × 45 mm — 600 × 750 pixels at 300 DPI. The tool above exports exactly 600 × 750 px.

How big should my head be in the photo?

Head and shoulders only; face height guided by the digital checker (the online service measures it for you) Use the grid overlay: position your eyes near the upper-middle line and keep your shoulders in the frame.

What background is required?

plain light grey or cream. The tool's background colour fills only transparent or empty areas — take the original photo against a real plain light grey or cream wall whenever possible; a digitally replaced background can be rejected if edges look cut.

What file size does the upload accept?

Digital: at least 600×750 px, jpeg, 50 kb to 10 mb; prints: 35×45 mm. The download reports its exact KB, so check it before submitting.

Can I print this at home?

Yes — the print-sheet button lays out 8 copies at exactly 35×45 mm on A4. Print at 100% scale (turn off 'fit to page') on matte photo paper.
